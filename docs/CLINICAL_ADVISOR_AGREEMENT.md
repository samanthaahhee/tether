## 1. Purpose

Hey Otis is building an AI-powered communication tool for couples in long-term relationships ("the Product"). The Product is a wellness app and is **explicitly not a medical device, therapy service, or crisis intervention service**.

Hey Otis is engaging the Advisor to provide independent clinical input on the Product's safety design, content, and operational protocols.

The Advisor is **not** providing therapy, treatment, diagnosis, or any form of clinical care to Hey Otis users.

The Advisor is **not** the responsible clinician for any user of the Product.

The Advisor is **not** providing medical, psychological, or psychiatric services to Hey Otis itself or to any individual.

## 2. Scope of work

The Advisor will provide the following:

### 2.1 Initial review (one-time)
- Review and provide written feedback on the Hey Otis *Safety & Guardrails Framework* document (current version: v1.0)
- Specifically review:
  - The 6 crisis category taxonomies and trigger patterns
  - The crisis response templates and country-specific resource lists
  - The cultural humility and identity respect language
  - The 4-step framework (Vent / Understand / Prepare / Nurture)
  - A representative sample of 20 sample LLM outputs

### 2.2 Ongoing reviews (quarterly)
- Quarterly review of:
  - All crisis flag events from the previous quarter
  - Output filter rejection patterns
  - Any user-reported safety incidents
  - Updates to crisis taxonomy as patterns emerge
- Estimated 2-3 hours per quarter

### 2.3 Reactive consultation (as-needed)
- On request from Hey Otis: ad-hoc input on specific clinical questions, escalations, or product changes
- Reasonable response time: 5 business days, except for safety-critical questions which require best-efforts faster response

### 2.4 Authorisation to be named
- The Advisor consents to being named as Hey Otis's Clinical Advisor on the Product, the website, the pitch deck, investor materials, and App Store listings, subject to clause 6 below.

## 4. Independence

The Advisor:
- Is engaged as an **independent contractor**, not an employee
- Provides **independent professional judgement**
- May decline to advise on any matter that conflicts with their professional ethics, licensing requirements, or registered scope of practice
- Is not obligated to endorse any aspect of the Product they have not reviewed
- May resign at any time on 30 days' written notice

## 5. Liability scope (critical — read carefully)

### 5.1 Advisor liability cap
The Advisor's total liability to Hey Otis under this agreement is capped at the total fees paid by Hey Otis to the Advisor in the 12 months preceding any claim. This cap survives termination.

### 5.2 No clinical liability for end users
The Advisor is **not in a clinical relationship** with any Hey Otis user. The Advisor:
- Does not see, treat, or advise individual users
- Does not have access to user data, conversations, or identifying information
- Has no clinical duty of care to any Hey Otis user
- Is not the prescribing, diagnosing, or treating clinician for any user

The Advisor's role is limited to **reviewing the Product's design, copy, and protocols** — not to providing care.

### 5.3 Hey Otis indemnity
Hey Otis indemnifies the Advisor against:
- Any claim brought by an end user of the Product alleging clinical harm, malpractice, or breach of duty of care, except where the claim arises from gross negligence or wilful misconduct of the Advisor
- Any claim by a third party alleging the Advisor is the responsible clinician for a Hey Otis user
- Reasonable legal costs for defending such claims

This indemnity is supported by Hey Otis's professional indemnity insurance policy (see clause 7).

### 5.4 No fitness-for-purpose warranty
The Advisor's review is a **professional opinion** based on best practices and current published guidelines at the time of review. The Advisor does not warrant that the Product is fit for any particular purpose, that it will prevent all harm, or that it complies with any specific regulatory regime — those determinations rest with Hey Otis and its legal counsel.

## 6. Use of name & credentials

Hey Otis may name the Advisor as Clinical Advisor on:
- The Product (in-app About / Settings)
- The Hey Otis website
- Investor materials and pitch decks
- App Store listings
- Press releases (with prior written approval)

Hey Otis may use the Advisor's credentials and short biography (drafted by Advisor or approved in writing by Advisor).

The Advisor may withdraw consent to be named on 30 days' written notice. If withdrawn, Hey Otis will remove the Advisor's name from all controllable surfaces within 30 days.

The Advisor's name and credentials may not be used to:
- Imply the Advisor endorses specific outputs or interactions of the Product
- Imply the Advisor reviewed any individual user's conversation
- Imply the Product is therapy, medical care, or a regulated medical device
- Suggest the Advisor is the responsible clinician for any user

## 7. Insurance

Hey Otis maintains professional indemnity insurance covering:
- General product liability
- Cyber liability
- Errors and omissions related to the Product

The policy explicitly extends to cover the activities of the Advisor performed under this agreement (additional insured status).

A copy of the certificate of insurance will be provided to the Advisor on request.

## 8. Confidentiality

The Advisor agrees to keep confidential:
- All non-public information about the Product, Hey Otis's business, finances, roadmap, users, and operations
- All data shared with the Advisor in the course of the engagement

Confidentiality survives termination of this agreement for [3] years, except for information that:
- Becomes public other than through the Advisor's breach
- Was known to the Advisor before disclosure
- Is required to be disclosed by law or professional licensing body

## 9. Intellectual property

- Pre-existing IP of each party remains with that party
- Work product created by the Advisor under this agreement (e.g. written feedback, taxonomy revisions) is owned by Hey Otis
- The Advisor retains the right to publish general academic or professional insights from the engagement, provided no Hey Otis confidential information is disclosed and Hey Otis is given 30 days' notice for review

## 10. Data protection

- The Advisor will not be given access to any personal data of Hey Otis users
- Where the Advisor reviews sample outputs, the samples will be either fully synthetic or fully de-identified
- The Advisor and Hey Otis are independent data controllers for any data they each hold
- Both parties comply with GDPR

## 11. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement on 30 days' written notice.

Upon termination:
- Hey Otis pays for work completed up to the termination date
- The Advisor's name is removed from all Hey Otis materials within 30 days (where Advisor was previously named)
- Confidentiality, indemnity, and IP provisions survive

## 12. Conflict of interest

The Advisor confirms that:
- They are not in clinical practice with any individual currently using or known to the Advisor to use the Product (or will recuse from review of any matter where this arises)
- They have no financial interest in any directly competing product
- They will disclose any potential conflict of interest as it arises

## 13. Governing law & dispute resolution

This agreement is governed by the laws of [the Netherlands].

Any dispute will first be addressed by good-faith negotiation between the parties. If unresolved within 60 days, disputes will be referred to mediation in [Amsterdam] before any litigation.

## 14. Whole agreement

This document constitutes the whole agreement between the parties on its subject matter, superseding any prior discussions or correspondence.

Variations require written agreement signed by both parties.
