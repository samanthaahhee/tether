# Hey Otis — Instagram Content Calendar (First 4 Weeks)

## Brand Voice
- Warm, empathetic, never preachy
- Speaks like a wise friend, not a therapist
- Uses "you" and "your partner" — personal, not clinical
- Short sentences. Emotional resonance over information density.

## Visual Style
- Brand green (#96D35F) as accent, cream (#F7F5FD) backgrounds
- Instrument Sans for headlines, Inter for body
- Soft gradients, rounded corners, clean whitespace
- Dog mascot (Otis) for personality
