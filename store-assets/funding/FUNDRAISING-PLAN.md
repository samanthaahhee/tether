## 2. Non-dilutive funding — step by step

This is where the Netherlands is genuinely world-class. Stack all four.

### 2.1 WBSO — R&D tax credit (do this Monday)

**What it is.** A payroll tax rebate of ~32% on R&D salary costs (up to €350k of R&D salary; then 16% above that). If you hire a developer on €60k/year, WBSO returns ~€19k to you via lower payroll tax.

**Eligible:** writing novel software that solves a technical uncertainty — this app qualifies. Your AI orchestration layer, privacy-partitioned data model, and personalisation engine all count.

**Fees:** none. Free to apply.

**Steps:**

1. Get **eHerkenning niveau 3** (digital business identity) at [eherkenning.nl](https://www.eherkenning.nl). Takes 1–2 weeks. Costs €20–75/year depending on provider (KPN, Digidentity, QuoVadis).
2. Register your BV with **RVO** at [mijn.rvo.nl](https://mijn.rvo.nl) using eHerkenning.
3. File form **"WBSO aanvraag"** via mijn.rvo.nl.
    - Project title, description (what's novel / what technical problem you're solving)
    - List of R&D activities (e.g., "design of privacy-preserving vent data partitioning", "AI prompt orchestration across 5 therapy frameworks")
    - Estimated R&D hours per person
    - Target period (must start *after* application; apply before each new period)
4. Application windows: rolling. **You must apply *before* the R&D starts.** File for your next 4–12 month period now.
5. RVO decides in ~3 months. Approval → monthly payroll tax credit.

**What you submit:**
- Project description (1–2 pages per project; template on RVO site)
- Team list + hours
- No financial statements needed

**Key tip:** keep an **hour log** per person per project. RVO can audit up to 5 years back. The "WBSO Administratie" excel template from RVO is sufficient.

---

### 2.2 Vroegefasefinanciering (VFF) — pre-revenue loan

**What it is.** A subordinated loan of **€50k–€350k** to take an idea from concept to product-market-fit. Success-based repayment — you only repay if the company succeeds. Functionally non-dilutive.

**Eligible:** innovative startups under 5 years old, validated in "vroege fase" (early phase), with a credible plan to reach PMF.

**Fees:** none to apply.

**Steps:**

1. Prerequisite: get a **"positive advice"** from one of the authorised evaluators:
    - **Business Angel** (must be on the RVO-registered list)
    - **Participation fund** (Peak, Rockstart, etc. can qualify)
    - **Regional Development Agency** (ROM Utrecht/Regionale Ontwikkelingsmaatschappij for your province)
2. With positive advice letter, submit **"VFF aanvraag"** via mijn.rvo.nl. Form requires:
    - Business plan (10–20 pages)
    - Financial forecast (3 years, monthly for year 1)
    - Innovation description (what's novel)
    - Team + advisors
    - Use-of-funds split
    - Repayment plan (success-case)
3. RVO reviews quarterly — expect **~3 months** from submission to decision.
4. If approved: loan tranches disbursed against milestones.

**Interest:** ~8% (set yearly). Repayment only from year 3 and only if you're viable.

**What you submit:** business plan + financial model + positive-advice letter.

**Key tip:** get the positive advice from a **ROM** (regional development agency) rather than an angel if possible — faster, no equity expectations. For Amsterdam / Noord-Holland: **Invest-NL** can issue advice. For Utrecht: **ROM Utrecht Region**. For Rotterdam / Zuid-Holland: **InnovationQuarter**.

### 2.3 MIT — feasibility or R&D voucher

**What it is.** Regional innovation grant. Two variants:
- **MIT Haalbaarheid (feasibility)** — up to **€20k (40% subsidy)** for a feasibility study. You pay 60%, they pay 40%. Max grant €20k ⇒ project up to €50k.
- **MIT R&D samenwerking** — up to **€200k (35% subsidy)** for an R&D collaboration between 2+ SMEs.

**Eligible:** SME, fits a "topsector" — for Hey Otis, that's **Life Sciences & Health (LSH)** or **Creative Industry**.

**Fees:** none.

**Steps:**

1. Windows open twice per year. 2026 likely windows: **~April** (feasibility) and **~June** (R&D). Check your province's page on [mijn.rvo.nl](https://mijn.rvo.nl).
2. Submission is **first-come-first-served** on opening day — prepare in advance and submit the instant it opens.
3. Required documents:
    - Project plan (6–10 pages): problem, approach, deliverables, budget
    - Activity schedule (Gantt)
    - Budget + external-service quotes
    - Topsector alignment statement
4. Decision: ~13 weeks after close.

**What you submit:** project plan + budget + quotes.

**Best use for Hey Otis:** use the feasibility grant to fund a **clinical pilot** — e.g. efficacy validation with a Dutch psychology research group. Unlocks €20k + credibility + paper you can cite.

### 2.4 Innovatiekrediet — bigger loan, later

**What it is.** A loan of **up to €5M** (25% repayment if you fail, full repayment + interest if you succeed). For clinical or technical innovation with real risk.

**Eligible:** post-launch, working product, clear technical or clinical risk remaining.

**Fees:** none to apply, but you'll want a funding consultant for this one.

**Timing for Hey Otis:** not now. Apply **Q3 2027** once you have efficacy data and a path to scale.

**What it is.** Up to **€2.5M grant + €15M equity co-investment** from the European Innovation Council. Highly competitive (~5% success rate), massive upside.

**Eligible:** post-launch, European scale ambition, deep tech or high-impact innovation. Mental health qualifies.

**Timing for Hey Otis:** **Q4 2027**. Need real traction (50k+ MAUs) and a clinical evidence base.

**Steps (for when you're ready):**

2. Step 2: Full business plan + commercial plan (~50 pages). Remote evaluation.
3. Step 3: In-person pitch in Brussels.
4. Total timeline: 9–12 months from Step 1 to funding.

**What you submit:** full application via [EU Funding & Tenders Portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal).

## 4. IP protection — what actually works

### The honest answer first

**You cannot stop another company from building a "couples repair app."** IP law doesn't protect ideas, concepts, or business models anywhere in the world. Paired can add a "vent" mode tomorrow. Lasting can copy the four-step flow. No legal instrument prevents this.

**What you CAN protect:**
- The **name** "Hey Otis" and the mascot (trademarks + design rights)
- The **code** (copyright, automatic)
- The **AI prompt architecture** (trade secret)
- The **clinical content + curated therapy mappings** (copyright + trade secret)
- The **data you collect** (database rights)
- The **category narrative** ("rupture to repair") — practically, via first-mover brand equity

**The real moat is execution:** data compounds, brand compounds, user habit compounds. Legal IP slows competitors by 6–18 months — that's the realistic ceiling.

### 4.1 Trademark — file this week

**Priority: critical. Cost: low. Time to value: immediate.**

#### Benelux (BX) — file first, most important for you

- **Office:** [Benelux Office for Intellectual Property (BOIP)](https://www.boip.int)
- **Cost:** **€244** for one class; **€27** per additional class; **€81** per additional class above 3.
- **Classes to file:** 
    - Class 9 (software)
    - Class 38 (communications)
    - Class 42 (software as a service)
    - Class 44 (medical / psychological services) — **important for the mental-health positioning**
- **Total for 4 classes:** ~€325
- **Timeline:** 4–6 months to registration; protection effective on filing date.
- **Filing:** online at boip.int. Need: logo file, goods/services description, applicant details.
- **What to register:**
    1. **Hey Otis** (word mark)
    2. **The Otis mascot** (figurative mark — upload PNG)
    3. **"From rupture to repair"** (tagline, word mark) — optional but cheap

#### EU (EUTM) — if you plan to expand beyond Benelux

- **Office:** [EUIPO (Alicante)](https://euipo.europa.eu)
- **Cost:** **€850** for one class; **€50** for second class; **€150** per class beyond two.
- **Total for 4 classes:** €1,200.
- **Timeline:** 4–5 months if no opposition.
- **Benefit:** covers all 27 EU states.
- **Recommendation:** file Benelux now; file EUTM in 6 months once Benelux is granted.

#### US (USPTO) — for the App Store / Play Store

- **Office:** [USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov) — file via TEAS Plus.
- **Cost:** **$250–350 per class** (current: $350 TEAS Plus / $550 TEAS Standard).
- **Timeline:** 10–12 months typical.
- **Recommendation:** file US trademark before App Store launch (protects the name on Apple's stores).

**Total trademark budget (NL + EU + US, 4 classes each):** ~€2,800.

### 4.2 Design rights — cheap protection for the mascot + UI

**Benelux Design (BOIP)**
- **Cost:** **€108** for one design, **€54** for each additional (up to 50 in one filing).
- **What to register:**
    1. Otis mascot full body
    2. Mascot expressions (nurture, vent, etc.)
    3. The 4-step UI layout (distinctive screen design)
- **Timeline:** registered in weeks.
- **Term:** 5 years, renewable up to 25 years.
- **Budget:** ~€200 for 3 designs.

**EU Community Design (EUIPO)**
- **Cost:** €350 for one design + publication fee.
- **Recommendation:** file Benelux first; upgrade to EU when you have budget.

### 4.3 Trade secrets — the STRONGEST tool for Hey Otis

**Why this matters most.** Your real IP is in the **prompt architecture** (how you fuse 5 frameworks into one voice), the **personalisation weights** (how assessment results shape responses), the **curated therapy content**, and the **clinical safety rails**. None of that is patentable in Europe. **All of it is protectable as a trade secret** under the Dutch **Wet bescherming bedrijfsgeheimen (WBb, 2018)** — the implementation of EU Directive 2016/943.

**Requirements to qualify:**
1. The information must be **secret** (not generally known or accessible).
2. It must have **commercial value** because it is secret.
3. You must take **reasonable steps to keep it secret**.

**Steps to implement (do all of these):**

1. **Internal access controls.**
    - Production secrets in a password manager (1Password/Bitwarden), role-based access.
    - Separate "production" and "development" environments.
    - Two-factor authentication on GitHub, Supabase, Anthropic, Vercel, Apple Developer, Google Play Console.
2. **NDAs on everyone who touches sensitive material.**
    - Every contractor, freelancer, designer, developer signs a **mutual NDA** before seeing prompts, data, or roadmap.
    - Template: use [Rocket Lawyer NL](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/nl) or have a lawyer draft once (~€500).
3. **IP assignment in every contract.**
    - Every employee/contractor signs a **"Akte van overdracht van IE-rechten"** (IP transfer deed) stating all work-product rights transfer to the BV.
    - In employment contracts: "**alle intellectuele eigendomsrechten** vallen toe aan werkgever".
4. **Confidentiality clauses in employment contracts** — 2-year post-termination confidentiality is standard and enforceable in NL.
5. **Non-compete clauses** — enforceable for max 1 year (Dutch law) if properly drafted.
6. **Document your trade secrets.**
    - Keep a **"Trade Secrets Register"** (private internal doc) listing what is claimed as a trade secret, when created, who has access.
    - This is what you'd show a court if someone leaks.
7. **Data room discipline for fundraising.**
    - When sharing with VCs: use Datasite, DocSend, or Notion with expiring links.
    - Watermark every document. Never email the prompt stack.

**No filing needed. Protection kicks in automatically if you meet the three conditions.**

### 4.4 Patents — don't waste money

**Reality check for Europe:**

- **European Patent Convention Article 52(2):** "programs for computers" are excluded from patentability "as such."
- To patent software in EU, you need a **"technical effect"** beyond the software itself (e.g., "improves computer efficiency"). "Better couples therapy outcomes" doesn't qualify.
- Patent cost: **€20–50k+ per patent** (drafting + examination + annuities over 20 years).
- Time: **3–5 years** to grant.
- Enforcement: ruinously expensive.

**Verdict:** skip software patents. Your money is 10× better spent on trademarks, trade-secret discipline, and brand-building.

**Exception:** if you ever develop a genuinely novel **technical** invention (e.g., a new privacy-preserving ML architecture), reconsider then.

### 4.5 Copyright — automatic, free

- Under Dutch **Auteurswet**, copyright is automatic upon creation of original work. No registration needed.
- Covers: source code, design files, marketing copy, mascot artwork, app copy, onboarding text.
- **To prove date of creation** (useful in disputes), deposit key assets with **BOIP i-DEPOT**: **€37 for 5 years**. Optional but cheap insurance.

### 4.6 Database rights — Dutch-specific protection

Under the **Databankenwet** (implementing EU Database Directive), your **user data + insights database** gets **sui generis protection**:
- 15-year protection from substantial investment in compiling/maintaining a database.
- Prevents others from extracting/re-using substantial parts.

**No filing needed.** Automatic if you can show "substantial investment" (which you can — you're paying for Anthropic API to build those insights).

**Action:** document your data-compilation process internally to establish the "substantial investment" threshold.

## 5. Structural & legal setup

### 5.1 Entity structure

**Recommended: Holding BV + Operating BV**

```
         YOU (natural person)
              │
              ▼
       Holding BV (100% owned by you)
              │
              ▼
       Hey Otis BV (operating company)
              │
              └─ Owns: IP, contracts, employees, cashflow
```

**Why:** 
- Dividends from Operating → Holding are tax-exempt (deelnemingsvrijstelling).
- If you ever sell Hey Otis BV, proceeds go to Holding tax-free, you pay only when withdrawing.
- Standard for any Dutch founder raising equity.

**Cost to set up:** notary fee **€800–1,500**. Recommended notaries: van Doorne, CMS, Clifford Chance for VC-experienced; or local notary €800 if basic.

**Timing:** set up before your first equity raise. No benefit to waiting.

### 5.2 Other legal basics

- **Register with KVK (Chamber of Commerce):** €82.25 one-time. Needed for the BV.
- **VAT registration:** automatic on KVK registration.
- **DGA (Director-Major Shareholder) salary:** minimum €56k/year (2026 figure) — relevant once you pay yourself.
- **Shareholders' agreement:** draft when you raise pre-seed. €2–5k legal fees.
- **Employment contracts:** use a NL-specialist lawyer or [HRmarks](https://www.hrmarks.nl) / [DLA Piper templates](https://www.dlapiper.com).

### 5.3 Clinical / regulatory

- **Medical device classification (MDR/EU):** low-risk mental-wellness app is generally **outside MDR scope** as long as you explicitly disclaim therapeutic claims. Your current "not a replacement for therapy" language is the right posture.
- **EU AI Act (enforceable 2026–2027):** mental-health AI **may** fall under "high risk" if it influences health decisions. Get a lawyer opinion memo now (~€2k) — Dutch firms with this expertise: **Bird & Bird**, **DLA Piper**, **De Brauw**.
- **GDPR:** you're already subject. Publish a privacy policy (you have one planned), appoint a **DPO** (can be outsourced for ~€500/month), maintain a **Register of Processing Activities (ROPA)**.
