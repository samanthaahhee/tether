### Description (4000 chars max)
Every couple argues. The ones that last learn how to repair.

Hey Otis is your private, AI-powered guide from rupture to repair. Process conflict, uncover what's really going on, and find the words that open doors.

HOW IT WORKS
Hey Otis walks you through four steps, grounded in decades of relationship research:

1. Vent — Let it all out in a completely private space. No one else will ever see this.
2. Understand — Discover what's really going on beneath the surface of the conflict.
3. Prepare — Find the right words so your partner hears you, not an attack.
4. Nurture — Have the conversation with a step-by-step guide for repair.

UNDERSTAND YOUR RELATIONSHIP
Through short assessments, Hey Otis maps how you and your partner experience love, handle conflict, and respond to stress. See your attachment styles, love languages, and conflict patterns side by side.

GROW TOGETHER
Every answer you and your partner give teaches Hey Otis something new about your relationship. Those insights shape every conversation, so the guidance gets more personalised over time.

BUILT ON SCIENCE
Hey Otis draws from five evidence-based therapeutic frameworks:
• Gottman Method — 40+ years of relationship research
• Emotionally Focused Therapy — the science of attachment
• Non-Violent Communication — express needs without blame
• Internal Family Systems — understand protective patterns
• Cognitive Behavioural Couples Therapy — reframe automatic thoughts

TOOLS FOR TOUGH MOMENTS
Access breathing exercises, grounding techniques, and relationship repair tools whenever you need them.

100% PRIVATE
Your conversations are yours. Your partner will never see what you share in your private sessions.

Hey Otis is not a replacement for professional therapy or counselling.
