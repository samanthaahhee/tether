# Hey Otis — Reel Scripts

Shot-by-shot scripts for the 5 reels in the first 10 posts. Filmable on iPhone + edited in CapCut. Total production time per reel: **60–90 minutes** once you've shot b-roll once.

**Shared production notes (read first):**

- **Dimensions:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16). In CapCut: New Project → 9:16.
- **Brand text style:** Instrument Sans Bold, letterSpacing -2%, lineHeight 108%. On dark bg use `#F7F5FD`; on cream use `#211E28`. Accent colours for emphasis: lime `#96D35F`, periwinkle `#92A6F4`, orange `#F67700`, purple `#BD57F2`.
- **Opening hook:** first 1.5 seconds decides whether someone keeps watching. Always lead with a question, a specific hour/moment, or a contrarian statement — never with "hi!" or a brand logo.
- **Captions:** always on. CapCut's auto-caption is accurate — run it first, then style manually (Instrument Sans Bold, bottom-third, drop shadow).
- **Audio:** use trending sounds when concept allows, original voiceover when concept is narrative. Record voiceover on iPhone Voice Memos app in a quiet room with your face close to the phone — it's better than any external mic for this style.
- **End card:** last 1s, black frame, small cream "Hey Otis" logo + "waitlist link in bio →" in Inter Medium 36pt.

## Reel #6 — You don't have an attachment style. You have a pattern under pressure.

**Total runtime: 30–35 seconds**
**Concept:** Contrarian take against attachment-Instagram. Voiceover-led. Single-background reel with kinetic typography. No faces.

### Voiceover script (~32 seconds, read slowly)

> "Attachment Instagram has convinced a generation of people they're broken in four categories.
>
> They're not.
>
> Anxious attachment isn't a personality. Avoidant isn't a verdict. Disorganised isn't a diagnosis.
>
> These are responses to conditions — and conditions change.
>
> The goal was never to know your label. The goal was to know what you need when the pressure is on.
>
> And to learn to ask for it without losing yourself."

### Visual direction

- **Background:** solid cream `#F7F5FD` for first 20s, then switch to ink `#211E28` for the final 10s.
- **Typography:** Instrument Sans Bold, large. Each line of the script appears on screen synced to the voice, one phrase at a time. Fade-in animation with slight upward drift (CapCut → Text → Animation → "Up Soft").
- **Emphasis colour:** "not" in the phrase "They're NOT" appears in lime `#96D35F`, large, held for a beat.

### Shot-by-shot

| # | Timing | VO text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:04 | "Attachment Instagram has convinced a generation…" | Cream bg. "ATTACHMENT / INSTAGRAM" in massive ink text, two lines |
| 2 | 0:04–0:07 | "…that they're broken in four categories." | Four labels appear: `anxious · avoidant · secure · disorganised`. Grey, small |
| 3 | 0:07–0:10 | "They're not." | All four labels X-through. The word **NOT** replaces them, massive lime |
| 4 | 0:10–0:14 | "Anxious attachment isn't a personality." | "isn't a personality" — italic ink |
| 5 | 0:14–0:17 | "Avoidant isn't a verdict." | "isn't a verdict" — italic ink |
| 6 | 0:17–0:20 | "Disorganised isn't a diagnosis." | "isn't a diagnosis" — italic ink |
| 7 | 0:20–0:22 | (beat) | Background transitions cream → ink |
| 8 | 0:22–0:26 | "These are responses to conditions — and conditions change." | Cream text on ink. "conditions change" in lime italic |
| 9 | 0:26–0:30 | "The goal was never to know your label." | ink bg, cream text |
| 10 | 0:30–0:34 | "It was to know what you need when the pressure is on." | "what you need" in lime, rest in cream |
| 11 | 0:34–0:36 | (hold) | End card: Hey Otis logo + `waitlist link in bio →` |

### Production notes

- Record voiceover with pace and space. Do NOT rush. Let "They're not." land with a full second of silence.
- No music under the voice — this one needs the silence. Add a very soft ambient pad at 15% volume if silence feels too stark.
- This reel is designed to generate **comments** (contrarian take). Engage every comment in the first 2 hours — that's the algorithm play.

## Reel #7 — The fight under the fight

**Total runtime: 20–28 seconds**
**Concept:** Visual poetry. A single narrative voiceover over close-up shots of mundane domestic objects. No faces. Zero lifestyle-content energy.

### Voiceover script (~22 seconds)

> "It's not about the dishes.
>
> It's about who gets to be tired. The sixteen times you did them first. The birthday you remembered and the one they didn't.
>
> It's about being the only one who sees the small things.
>
> It's never about what it's about."

### Shot list

| # | Timing | Visual | VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Close-up of a single dirty dish in a sink, soft morning light | "It's not about the dishes." |
| 2 | 0:03–0:07 | Tilt up to a mug on a drying rack, slow pan | "It's about who gets to be tired." |
| 3 | 0:07–0:10 | A calendar on the wall, a date circled in pencil | "The birthday you remembered…" |
| 4 | 0:10–0:13 | Same calendar, next month, blank | "…and the one they didn't." |
| 5 | 0:13–0:16 | Close-up of a hand straightening a picture frame | "It's about being the only one who sees the small things." |
| 6 | 0:16–0:20 | Shot of the whole kitchen from a doorway, peaceful but lonely | (pause) |
| 7 | 0:20–0:24 | Text over the kitchen shot: **It's never about what it's about.** Instrument Sans Bold, cream, large, centered | "It's never about what it's about." |
| 8 | 0:24–0:28 | End card | |

### What to shoot

- One dirty dish in a clean sink
- A mug on a drying rack
- A calendar on a wall (prop one if you don't have one)
- A hand straightening a picture frame
- Kitchen from a doorway, still life

All shootable in 15 minutes in your own kitchen.

### Audio

- Use a trending ambient/cinematic sound from CapCut.
- Voiceover on top. Keep VO volume 3× higher than music.

### Why this will work

Everyone in a long relationship has had THIS fight. It's the most relatable frame in the entire niche. Tell it honestly and without skits, and it becomes send-to-your-partner content.

## Reel #9 — The 7 minutes before you walk into the conversation

**Total runtime: 45–55 seconds**
**Concept:** Countdown ritual. A person preparing for a hard conversation using Hey Otis. Timer visible on screen. Zero dialogue.

### Visual arc

Background is a real kitchen or living room, golden-hour light. Main subject: hands on a phone (Hey Otis open), preparing tea, exhaling. We never see a face. The clock counts DOWN from 07:00 to 00:00.

### Shot list

| # | Timing | On-screen timer | Visual | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:04 | — | Black screen. Sound of a deep exhale | **07:00** fades in, massive, centered, Instrument Sans Bold |
| 2 | 0:04–0:08 | 07:00 | Hand putting a kettle on the stove | — |
| 3 | 0:08–0:14 | 06:38 | Hand opening Hey Otis on the phone (Vent screen), scrolling into view | *Vent* (lime, Instrument Sans Italic, bottom-left) |

| 6 | 0:25–0:30 | 03:18 | Close-up of hand lifting the kettle, steam | — |
| 7 | 0:30–0:35 | 02:47 | Switch to Prepare screen, text being drafted | *Prepare* (orange, italic) |
| 8 | 0:35–0:40 | 01:54 | Nurture screen, with step-by-step guide visible, scrolling through | *Nurture* (purple, italic) |

### Production notes

- **The timer is the hero.** Make it massive. Animate it counting down smoothly. In CapCut: use the "Countdown Timer" effect OR manually update text every 2s.
- Music: build slowly. Use an ambient track that has a subtle crescendo toward the end. "Max Richter — On the Nature of Daylight" works but it's overused — find a less-known instrumental.
- Film the Hey Otis screens on a real device if possible. If the app isn't far enough along, mock up screens in Figma, screen-record scrolling through them, and film a hand-on-phone with those mockups playing.

## Reel #10 — The hardest sentence in a relationship

**Total runtime: 25–30 seconds**
**Concept:** Build to a single sentence. Voiceover-led. Typography ends the reel and becomes the shareable asset.

### Voiceover script (~25 seconds)

> "It's not 'I love you.'
>
> It's not 'I'm leaving.'
>
> It's not even 'I'm sorry.'
>
> The hardest sentence in a long relationship is this:
>
> *'I don't know how to say this without hurting you, but I need to try.'*
>
> Say that one, and the conversation you've been avoiding for three weeks becomes possible.
>
> Don't say it, and the small resentments become the wallpaper of your life."

### Shot list

All frames are background cream `#F7F5FD` with ink `#211E28` typography, EXCEPT the final hero sentence which inverts.

| # | Timing | VO line | On-screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | (silence, build anticipation) | *"Three words most couples never learn to say."* Tiny italic, bottom third, cream bg |
| 2 | 0:03–0:06 | "It's not 'I love you.'" | **I love you** (massive, cream bg, grey text, with a subtle strike-through) |
| 3 | 0:06–0:09 | "It's not 'I'm leaving.'" | **I'm leaving** (strike through) |
| 4 | 0:09–0:12 | "It's not even 'I'm sorry.'" | **I'm sorry** (strike through) |
| 5 | 0:12–0:15 | "The hardest sentence in a long relationship is this:" | Fade to black. "this:" in lime, large |
| 6 | 0:15–0:22 | "I don't know how to say this without hurting you, but I need to try." | **Full sentence appears word-by-word over 5 seconds.** Cream on ink, Instrument Sans Bold 64pt, centered. Hold for 3 extra seconds on the complete sentence |
| 7 | 0:22–0:27 | "Say that one, and the conversation becomes possible." | Sentence stays. Small italic above: *"say that one →"* |
| 8 | 0:27–0:30 | (music resolves) | End card: Hey Otis logo + `waitlist link in bio →` |

### Production notes

- The **word-by-word reveal** of the hero sentence is THE moment. Time it so each word lands on a beat of music.
- Music: something with a single building note that resolves. Ambient piano works.
- This is the single most-shareable reel in the set because the sentence itself is the thing people send to their partner.

# Filming workflow — produce all 5 reels in 2 days

**Day 1 (Saturday morning, 3 hours) — shoot all b-roll:**

For reels #2, #7, #9 (object-driven cinematic reels), shoot all the b-roll in one session:
- Kettle, mugs, doors, lamps, dishes, calendars, phones, hands
- Shoot each clip for 8–10 seconds at 4K 30fps (higher than you'll use — gives editing flexibility)
- Shoot in golden hour where possible (morning 7–9am, evening 6–8pm)
- Keep the phone horizontal while shooting — you'll crop to 9:16 in edit

You'll reuse this b-roll library for FUTURE reels too. Shoot more than you think you need.

**Day 1 evening (1 hour) — record all voiceovers:**

For reels #6, #7, #9, #10 — record all voiceovers in one session while your voice is consistent. Use iPhone Voice Memos in a carpeted room with the phone 6 inches from your mouth. Read each script 3 times and keep the best take.

**Day 2 (Sunday, 3–4 hours) — edit all 5 reels in CapCut:**

Spend 40 minutes per reel:
- Import b-roll + voiceover
- Cut to timing
- Add text overlays per the shot lists
- Add trending / ambient audio
- Auto-caption with CapCut's AI captioner
- Export at 4K, 60fps if available

**Total time: 7–8 hours for 5 finished reels.** Post them across 2 weeks per the content calendar.
