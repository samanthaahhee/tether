## Week 6 · New carousel — A SOFT START-UP, FROM SCRATCH (8 slides)

**Concept:** Gottman's "soft start-up" framework. Takes a harsh start-up ("You NEVER…") and rewrites it in real time, line by line.

**Slides:**
1. Hook — "Your first sentence decides the next hour. Make it a soft start-up."
2. The harsh version — "You NEVER think about how tired I am. You just expect me to do everything."
3. "Step 1: Remove 'never' and 'always'." — rewrite attempt 1
4. "Step 2: Start with 'I', not 'you'." — rewrite attempt 2
5. "Step 3: Name a specific moment, not a pattern." — rewrite attempt 3
6. "Step 4: Ask for something concrete." — rewrite attempt 4
7. The final soft start-up — "Last night when I came home and saw the dishes from this morning, I felt really alone. Can we figure out a rhythm that doesn't leave me feeling like the only one who notices?"
8. Closer — "Hey Otis writes one of these with you, line by line. Waitlist →"

**Caption:**
A soft start-up isn't just "being nice." It's a specific shape. Four rules:

1. No "never" or "always."
2. Start with "I," not "you."
3. Name a specific moment, not a pattern.
4. Ask for something concrete.

Get those four right and you've changed the next hour of your relationship before you've said the second sentence.

# Measurement & iteration

**What to watch each week:**
- Saves per post (best single signal of content-market fit)
- Shares to DMs (emotional content = DM share; algorithm rewards this)
- Comments-per-reach ratio (target: >1%)
- Profile visits from post (leading indicator of waitlist conversion)
- Waitlist signup attribution (add `?src=ig` to the waitlist URL via UTMs)

**Kill criteria:** any post with <0.5% save rate after 48h means the hook didn't land. Don't repeat that exact format.

**Amplify signal:** when something saves well, make its sequel. Save magnets spawn save magnet series.

## Hashtag rotation discipline

Across these 32 posts I've varied hashtag sets deliberately. IG's algorithm penalises identical sets across consecutive posts. Rules:

1. Never reuse more than 5 hashtags from your most recent post.
2. Keep 5–7 "anchor" hashtags you rotate into most sets: `#couples #relationships #couplestherapy #therapytools #softstartup #emotionalintelligence #heyotis`
3. Vary the other 8–10 by post topic specificity.
