# Screenshot Copy — App Store & Google Play

Captions shown above each phone mockup in the store listings. Same copy for iOS and Android; only image dimensions differ (see each store's README).

**Voice rules** — warm, grounded, second-person. No clinical jargon. No exclamation marks. Use the in-app phrases wherever possible so the store matches the product.

**Design rules** — Headline in the app's display font, ~72pt, left-aligned. Subheadline ~32pt, 80% opacity. Each panel is colour-coded to the step or tab being shown so the sequence reads as a journey.

---

## The 8-screenshot sequence

Screens 2–5 (the four steps) are the non-negotiable core — they tell the whole product story on their own. If Apple/Google limits you to 5, cut screen 7 and screen 8.

### 1. Hero — *Home screen*
**From rupture to repair.**
Your private, AI-powered guide through the fights that matter.

*Screen:* Home tab with Otis mascot and the lime "Start a new session" button.
*Colour:* Cream background, lime accent.

---

### 2. Vent — *Step 1*
**Let it all out.**
A private space to say the thing you can't say out loud. Your partner will never see it.

*Screen:* Vent chat with the green step badge, one or two user messages in the chat.
*Colour:* Lime green (`#96d35f`).

---

### 3. Understand — *Step 2*
**See what's really going on.**
Otis helps you find the feeling underneath the fight — and what you actually need.

*Screen:* Understand chat with the blue step badge, a reflective Otis message visible.
*Colour:* Periwinkle blue (`#92a6f4`).

---

### 4. Prepare — *Step 3*
**Find words they can hear.**
Turn raw feelings into clear, fair language. No attack, no blame — just the truth.

*Screen:* Prepare chat with the orange step badge, a drafted "soft start-up" visible.
*Colour:* Warm orange (`#f67700`).

---

### 5. Nurture — *Step 4*
**Have the conversation.**
A step-by-step guide to open well, stay grounded, and close with care.

*Screen:* Nurture bridge card with the four numbered guides (opening line, if things get heated, what you need, how to close well).
*Colour:* Purple (`#bd57f2`).

---

### 6. Grow together — *Learnings → Together tab*
**Grow, together.**
See how you both love, fight, and repair — side by side.

*Screen:* Together tab with both avatars connected by the heart bridge, plus one relationship pattern card.
*Colour:* Cream with soft gradient.

---

### 7. Tools — *Tools tab*
**Tools for tough moments.**
Breathe. Ground. Reset — in 60 seconds or less.

*Screen:* Box-breathing animation mid-exercise, or the grounding techniques list.
*Colour:* Calm blue/teal.

---

### 8. Science — *Frameworks screen*
**Built on decades of research.**
Gottman, EFT, NVC, IFS, CBT — in your pocket, on your terms.

*Screen:* Frameworks page with the five cards visible.
*Colour:* Cream with multi-colour framework accents.

## What NOT to say

- ❌ "AI-powered relationship coach" (reads like marketing filler)
- ❌ "Revolutionary" / "Game-changing" / any superlative
- ❌ "Save your relationship" (implies it's broken — off-voice)
- ❌ "Couples therapy in your pocket" (legally risky; we're explicit we're not therapy)
- ❌ Exclamation marks anywhere
